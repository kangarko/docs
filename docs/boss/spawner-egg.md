# Spawner Egg

![Spawner Egg Banner](/images/boss/atA93N1.png)

Wouldn't it be fun if you could spawn your bosses where you want, and how you want it? That's why we added a special feature named Spawner Eggs, which allows you to summon Bosses easily.

## Obtaining a Spawner Egg
Grab one from the Boss menu, or run `/boss egg <boss> [player] [amount]` to hand a stack to someone.

Change how the egg looks under Boss menu > Spawning > **Spawn Egg Appearance**: material, name and lore.

![Boss Menu Spawner Egg](/images/boss/2R0X6CV.png)

## Using a Spawner Egg
Right click to spawn the Boss (tip: You can also click the air or blocks far away!). Left click to open Boss menu.

The egg is consumed unless you are in Creative. Tameable Bosses are tamed to whoever spawned them. Spawning inside another player's GriefPrevention claim is blocked unless you have `boss.bypass.claim`.

Boss eggs also work in dispensers. The Boss appears at the block the dispenser faces and one egg is used up.

![Using Spawner Egg](/images/boss/hmXEiG9.png)

## Air Spawn
This is a special function allowing you to spawn mobs in the distance out of your reach, which means that you can right click with the spawner egg blocks far away, or even air, and the Boss will be spawned there!

![Air Spawn](/images/boss/yf2P4pf.png)
